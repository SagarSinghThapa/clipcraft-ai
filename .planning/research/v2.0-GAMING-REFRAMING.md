## 1. Streamer Facecam Localization & Extraction

### Challenge
In gaming content (e.g., GTA, Valorant, Apex, reactions), a streamer facecam is embedded inside 16:9 widescreen gameplay. Webcams vary in size (typically 12% to 25% of screen width) and position (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right).

### Solution: Spatial Cluster Frame Sampling
1. **Low-Frequency Sampling:** Sample 1 frame every 2 seconds across the video (cheap: ~150 frames for a 5-minute video, ~500 frames for 30 minutes).
2. **Face Bounding Box Detection:** Run OpenCV Haar cascade or DNN face detector on sampled frames.
3. **Spatial Persistence Clustering:**
   - Gameplay faces (NPCs, cutscenes) appear transiently and move across the screen.
   - Streamer faces appear persistently in the same screen quadrant across >60% of sampled frames.
   - Compute spatial bounding box cluster: find the median `(cx, cy, fw, fh)` of detections in the densest spatial cluster.
4. **Facecam Region Definition:**
   - Expand the face bounding box by 1.8x width and 1.6x height to capture shoulders, microphone, and gestures while preserving head room.
   - Constrain aspect ratio to standard widescreen (16:9) or landscape box.
   - Clamp coordinates within `[0, video_width]` and `[0, video_height]`.
   - Result: Exact persistent webcam box `(cam_x, cam_y, cam_w, cam_h)`.

## 2. Content-Aware Gameplay Cropping

### Challenge
When converting 16:9 gameplay to vertical, cropping the center can cut off minimaps, health bars, vehicle chases, or crosshairs.

### Solution: Activity-Weighted Crop Window
1. **Exclude Facecam Box:** Mask out the detected facecam region so streamer movement does not bias gameplay focus.
2. **Motion / Visual Activity Heatmap:**
   - Compute frame differencing / gradient magnitude across horizontal sectors (left, center, right).
   - For driving/chase games (GTA), action is centered or aligned with road direction.
   - For FPS/action games, crosshair and central combat zone have highest motion.
3. **Smooth Horizontal Trajectory:**
   - Use Exponential Moving Average (EMA, alpha=0.10) to slide the gameplay crop window `X` smoothly.
   - Prevent abrupt jumping between cuts.

## 3. Stacked Dual-Stream FFmpeg Filtergraph

### Architecture
A single horizontal video file is split, independently cropped, scaled, and vertically stacked via FFmpeg:

```
                  ┌─► [crop facecam] ──► [scale to 1080x720] ──┐
[input 1920x1080] ┤                                            ├─► [vstack] ──► [1080x1920]
                  └─► [crop gameplay] ─► [scale to 1080x1200] ─┘
```

### FFmpeg Filtergraph Specification:
```bash
ffmpeg -i source.mp4 -filter_complex \
  "[0:v]split=2[cam_in][game_in]; \
   [cam_in]crop={cam_w}:{cam_h}:{cam_x}:{cam_y},scale=1080:720:flags=lanczos[cam_out]; \
   [game_in]crop={game_w}:{game_h}:{game_x}:{game_y},scale=1080:1200:flags=lanczos[game_out]; \
   [cam_out][game_out]vstack=inputs=2[v_stacked]" \
  -map "[v_stacked]" -map 0:a -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset fast -c:a copy output_stacked.mp4
```

**Resolution Flexibility:**
- 1080x1920 (FHD): Top 1080x720, Bottom 1080x1200.
- 1440x2560 (2K): Top 1440x960, Bottom 1440x1600.
- 2160x3840 (4K): Top 2160x1440, Bottom 2160x2400.

## 4. Intelligent Caption Placement & Karaoke Animations

### Safe Placement Zones:
- **`LOWER_CENTER` (Default for Gaming Split):** Y=740 to Y=880 (positioned right at the visual seam below the streamer facecam and above gameplay action).
- **Benefits:**
  - 100% clear of streamer's face.
  - 100% clear of bottom TikTok / Shorts UI buttons (like/comment/share icons which occupy bottom 250px).
  - Maximizes viewer readability without obstructing gameplay minimap.

### Animated Word Karaoke Highlighting:
- Advanced SubStation Alpha (`.ass`) syntax supports inline styling overrides:
  `Dialogue: 0,0:00:01.20,0:00:01.80,Karaoke,,0,0,0,,{\c&H0000FFFF&}LOOK {\c&H00FFFFFF&}AT THAT CAR!`
- The spoken word dynamically illuminates in neon yellow/gold while remaining words stay white with dark borders.

## 5. Reaction-Aware Audio Energy Scoring

### Audio Signal Extraction:
1. Sample RMS amplitude across extracted 16kHz audio in 500ms windows.
2. Detect dynamic range expansion:
   $$\text{Reaction Surge} = \frac{\text{RMS}(t) - \mu_{\text{RMS}}}{\sigma_{\text{RMS}}}$$
3. Shouting, laughing, sudden screams, and gasp moments produce positive z-score spikes (>2.5 $\sigma$).
4. Gaming highlights combine:
   $$\text{Final Score} = 0.60 \times \text{Transcript Score} + 0.40 \times \text{Audio Reaction Score}$$
